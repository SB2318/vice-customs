# 🌴 Beginner ("Noob") Guide: What Can You Do in Vice Customs?

Here is a simple step-by-step guide to get started right away.

## 1. 🏎️ Choose Your Vehicle

At the top bar, click **INFERNUS**, **BANSHEE**, **DOMINATOR**, or **DIRTBIKE**.

Watch the interactive 3D model swap inside the Vice City garage!

---

## 2. 🎨 Paint & Finish Your Ride

Open the **Paint & Finish** panel tab.

### Primary Color

Choose from retro colors such as:

* Hot Pink
* Neon Cyan
* Cyber Yellow
* Midnight Black
* And more

### Dual-Tone Secondary

Use the secondary color to customize:

* Bonnet / hood section
* Side sill accents

### Finish Material

Choose between:

* **Gloss**
* **Matte**
* **Metallic**
* **Pearlescent**
* **Chameleon Iridescent**
* **Carbon Fiber**
* **Rust Patina**

---

## 3. 🏁 Drag & Drop Decals, Flames, Stripes & Stencils

Go to the **Decals Studio** tab.

### Racing Stripes

Choose from designs such as:

* Dual GT
* Offset Rally
* Checkered Flags

### Flames & Graphics

Add graphics such as:

* Hot-rod retro flames
* Cyber wings
* Vice palm trees

### Spray Stencils

Try designs such as:

* `#88 Vice Special`
* Aerosol palms
* Garage skull & wrenches

### Sponsor Logos

Add logos such as:

* Pegassi
* Grotti
* Sprunk
* eCola
* Atomic Tires

You can also add **Bullet Holes**.

### ✋ Move Your Decals

Use the **2D canvas control handles** to:

* Drag decals
* Scale decals
* Rotate decals

---

## 4. 🔤 Custom Typography & License Plates

Go to the **Text & Plates** tab.

### Custom Text

Type your own text, for example:

```text
VICE CITY
GT-88
```

Available fonts include:

* *Orbitron*
* *Impact*
* *Marker*
* *8-Bit Arcade*

### Custom License Plates

Create custom plates such as:

```text
VC 1986
V8 BEAST
```

Choose from styles including:

* Vice Sunset Pink
* Blue / Yellow
* Outlaw Black & Gold

---

## 5. 🖌️ Open Unlayer Image Editor

Click **🖌️ UNLAYER EDITOR** in the top header.

Use Unlayer's editor tools to:

* Apply custom filters
* Crop templates
* Add stickers
* Make freehand annotations

When you're finished, click **Save** to render the result onto your 3D car.

---

## 6. 🔊 Rev Engine, Underglow, Radio & Camera Views

### 🔥 Rev the Engine

Click **REV ENGINE!** to hear:

* V8 engine rumble
* Turbo spooling
* Blow-off valve hiss
* Backfire pops

### ⚡ Toggle Underglow

Click **⚡** to toggle the neon underglow lights.

### 📷 Camera Views

Switch between different camera angles:

* **3/4 Front**
* **Side**
* **Rear**
* **Spin 🔄** — 360° turntable

### 📻 Radio Stations

Play different radio stations from the bottom of the interface:

* **FLASH FM**
* **WAVE 103**
* **V-ROCK**
* **WILDSTYLE**

---

## 7. 📸 Export & Save

### 🎨 Presets

Click **PRESE**
